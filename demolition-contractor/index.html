A practical checklist before the work

Use these points to get ready for a project conversation:

  • Explain what you want to change or accomplish on the property.
  • Share any details you know about the site and the areas involved.
  • Ask what work is included and what will remain afterward.
  • Bring up flooring or other property work that may connect to the demolition.

Don't assume that asking to remove one feature includes nearby concrete or other attached elements. Don't leave the future use of the area out of the discussion, either.
